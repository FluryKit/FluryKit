You may notice that my IDR prices are lower when converted from USD. This is intentional.

I know AI-generated images are everywhere nowadays but its been especially difficult for artists here in Indonesia. Appreciation for real artists is still very very awfully low and even many official institutions choose AI instead of supporting local artists.

Thats one of the reasons I decided to offer special IDR pricing. I want to make my commissions fair and affordable for Indonesians while still respecting the value of my work and the art market. Id love to make art for my own people too, who still support real artists ^^

Because of this, the IDR prices are exclusively for commissioners residing in Indonesia. Please dont take advantage of the local pricing if you're commissioning from outside the country.

To everyone who commissions me using the USD pricing, thank you so much TwT Your support truly helps me with my daily living expenses, college, my family and my mom's medical care. It also allows me to continue offering affordable local pricing for Indonesian commissioners. Your kindness genuinely means more to me than you know ♡

✦ How to Place an Order ✦

You can place your order by clicking the button on the commission listing/card you want above, which will take you to your preferred platform. Once payment is confirmed, your commission will be added to my commission queue.

For local (IDR) payments, please order through the Google Form option. Make sure you have one of the payment methods listed below. I'll communicate with you in Indonesian using casual/informal/slang language to keep things comfortable for both of us! :D

VGen

Best for structured commission orders through my main commission system. Recommended if you prefer using VGen’s built-in ordering and payment process.

Ko-fi

Alternative option for commissions if you prefer Ko-fi’s payment system or already use Ko-fi for support. Works the same for commission requests, just through a different platform.

Google Form

Best for clients who don't use VGen or Ko-fi, or prefer direct PayPal invoices or local IDR e-wallet payments. Suitable for fully custom or off-platform requests.

Terms of Service

Credit & Usage

  • Credit is required when using my art whenever possible.
  • Do not claim my designs as your own. Always credit me as the original designer.

Turnaround Time

  • Commission time depends on my current queue length.
  • Once I have reached your request and started working on it, it usually takes 1–2 weeks or less to complete.
  • During high demand, it may take up to 1 month even after your request has been reached.
  • In cases of personal issues, emergencies or very complex commissions, it may take longer.
  • I will always keep you updated during the process and you’re welcome to ask for progress updates anytime!! :D

Communication

  • Please check your notifications regularly for updates.
  • I will not continue working on a commission without confirmation at required stages, which may affect completion time.
  • If there is no response for 6 months after multiple contact attempts and the commission has already been paid or started, it will be canceled with no refund.
  • If payment has not been made yet and there is no response for 2 weeks, the request will be canceled.

Refunds & Cancellations

  • If payment has not been made yet, you may cancel your request at any time!
  • If I am unable to continue due to serious personal emergencies or situations where I cannot work at all, refunds will be given depending on progress. If no work has been started, a full refund will be given.
  • Once payment is made and the commission is confirmed, the client is responsible for their own financial decision and refunds will not be issued.

Changes & Revisions

  • Some commissions may not allow major changes, so please read the commission details carefully before ordering.
  • Minor fixes after completion are allowed (such as small color mistakes or missing details).

Design Rules

  • Please do not heavily modify the design. Minor edits are fine as long as the original design is still recognizable and credited.
  • Please do not create designs that are identical or very similar to my original designs.
  • Breedables and offspring are allowed and may naturally share similarities with parent designs.
  • Please do not increase the design’s worth unless additional artwork has been added.

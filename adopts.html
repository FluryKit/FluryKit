✦ Adoptable & Design Terms of Service ✦

Credit & Ownership

  • Credit is required when using my art whenever possible.
  • Do not claim my designs as your own. Always credit me as the original designer.
  • An active Toyhouse account is required for character file transfers.

Design Usage & Edits

  • Please do not heavily modify the design. Minor edits are fine as long as the original design is still recognizable and credited.
  • Please do not create designs that are identical or very similar to my original designs.
  • Breedables and offspring are allowed and may naturally share similarities with parent designs.

Value & Reselling

  • You may trade, gift, or resell the design.
  • Please do not increase the design's worth unless additional paid artwork has been added.

Claims, Invoices & Backing Out Rules

  • All USD transactions are strictly sent via PayPal Invoice only.
  • Invoices must be paid within 24–48 hours after receiving the PayPal invoice.
  • If a design is Pending, back-up claims are welcomed! If the main buyer backs out, I will reach out to the next person in line.
  • Backing out or withdrawing a bid/offer without a valid reason will result in a 6-month soft ban from claiming designs.
  • Accumulating 5 back-outs total or engaging in deliberate trolling will result in a permanent blacklist from purchasing my adoptables.

Auctions & Offers (OTA)

  • Sniper Rule: Auctions end 24 hours after the last bid placed. Each new bid extends the timer by 24 hours.
  • Offers for OTAs/Auctions are logged publicly so other buyers can view active offers before making theirs.

Gallery Retention

  • Adopted designs remain in the gallery for weeks before being archived to keep the gallery clean.
